# Agent Manifest

## Identity

- **Name**: Kavi
- **Version**: 1.3.0
- **Description**: Kavi is a voice calendar and people assistant for Rokid Glasses. The wearer says "Kavi" to start, then asks about their day in plain language, and Kavi reads their own Google Calendar — which they authorize once on their phone — and renders the answer as a heads-up card while speaking a one-breath summary. It also remembers people: it photographs whoever is in front of the wearer and recalls their name, notes, and any meeting they share today. To link an account, say "Kavi sign in" (or "Kavi đăng nhập").
- **Author**: people-memory

## Capabilities

- **Permissions**:
  - camera
  - microphone
  - network
  - audio
  - storage
