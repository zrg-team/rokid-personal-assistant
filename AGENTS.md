# Agent Manifest

## Identity

- **Name**: Kavi
- **Version**: 1.5.0
- **Description**: Kavi is a voice calendar and people assistant for Rokid Glasses. The wearer says "Kavi" to start, then asks about their day in plain language, and Kavi reads their own Google Calendar — which they authorize once on their phone — and renders the answer as a heads-up card while speaking a one-breath summary. It also remembers people: it photographs whoever is in front of the wearer and recalls their name, notes, and any meeting they share today. Every command opens with the wake word: "Kavi start" ("Kavi bắt đầu") to sign in, "Kavi sync" ("Kavi cập nhật") to pick the sign-in up after authorizing on the phone, "Kavi calendar …" or "Kavi gmail …" to use a connected service, "Kavi halo" ("Kavi xin chào") to recall whoever is in front of the wearer, and "Kavi remember …" ("Kavi nhớ …") to store something about them.
- **Author**: people-memory
